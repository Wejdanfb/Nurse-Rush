HOW TO PLAY

Your shift

Run for 120 seconds, answer 2 SBAR questions correctly, and reach the finish line to survive the shift.

Touch

Swipe left / right — change lane
Swipe up — jump
Swipe down — slide

Keyboard

← → change lane
↑ / Space jump
↓ slide

Questions

Two questions appear mid-run. You get 30 seconds each, and that time comes out of your 120. A wrong or missed answer ends the shift immediately. A correct answer is +100.

Collect

Coins, cash, stethoscopes, first aid kits and medical supplies all add score.

Obstacles

Ambulances, beds, wheelchairs, carts, oxygen cylinders and equipment boxes cost points and slow you down — they do not end the run.
